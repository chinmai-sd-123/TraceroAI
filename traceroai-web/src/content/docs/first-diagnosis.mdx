Open any trace in the dashboard. The page reads from the top down, from what went
wrong to the evidence for it.

## The verdict

Under the question you'll see the probable cause, for example **Retrieval miss**, with
the reason in a sentence. When the LLM judge decided, the reason is the judge's own
words; for an unsupported claim it quotes the claim the context doesn't back, such as
`Unsupported: "On the request path, middlewares run from innermost to outermost."`

Next to it is a mark that says who decided:

- **Final**: the LLM judge's verdict.
- **Preliminary**: an estimate from term overlap or embedding similarity, used until
  the judge answers, or when no judge is configured.

[Estimates and judge verdicts](/docs/concepts/estimates-and-verdicts) explains the
difference and why it matters.

## Findings by stage

Three columns, one per stage: Retrieval (did the context match the question?),
Grounding (is every claim supported by that context?) and Answer (does it address the
question?). Each shows the check's result, its reason, and who decided. The column the
diagnosis blames is tinted red and tagged "Probable cause". Selecting a column opens
that step below.

## The execution

Every step in order, drawn to time where the SDK measured it: the query, retrieval,
the prompt, generation, evaluation, feedback and the raw trace. Select a step to see
what went in and what came out: for retrieval, each chunk with its rank, source and
score; for generation, the model, parameters, tokens and cost.

## What to do next

- If the retrieval column failed, look at the chunks: was the right document there?
- If grounding failed, compare the unsupported claim with the chunks.
- If it's part of a recovery run, the strip at the top shows every attempt and what
  changed between them.

[Investigate failures](/docs/fix/investigate) covers going from one trace to the fix.
