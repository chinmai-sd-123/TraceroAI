# Code of conduct

TraceroAI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep criticism about
the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language
aren't accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately through
their GitHub profile ([@chinmai-sd-123](https://github.com/chinmai-sd-123)). Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block
participants who don't follow it.
